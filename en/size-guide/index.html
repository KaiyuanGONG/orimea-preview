Find your size

Two figures are enough: your wrist and the fit you like. The Atelier then works out the number of beads.

Measure your wrist

  1. Wrap a strip of paper or a soft tape measure around your wrist, just above the bone.
  2. Mark where the strip meets itself, without pulling tight.
  3. Measure the length flat, in centimetres, to the nearest half centimetre.

Choose the fit

Snug · +0.8 cm
The bracelet stays in place on the wrist.
Comfort · +1.2 cm
Our recommendation: a little movement without sliding onto the hand.
Loose · +1.8 cm
The bracelet moves freely for a more relaxed look.
