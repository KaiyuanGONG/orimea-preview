What we commit to

  • Name every material exactly: natural, cultured, glass or ceramic.
  • State known treatments (dyeing, stabilising) as soon as samples are in hand.
  • Attribute no effect on health, luck or mood to our stones.
  • Show honest visualisations now, then real photographs as soon as the first samples arrive.

About the images

The images on this site are 3D renders computed from each material’s dimensions and optical properties. A natural stone stays unique: colour, clarity and inclusions vary from bead to bead.

Care

Take the bracelet off to swim, shower or exercise. Keep perfume and creams away from it. Store it flat, out of direct light.
