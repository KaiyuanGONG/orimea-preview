Collections

Each line has its own material, rhythm and starting price. In the Atelier you can start from any of them.

  1. Mineral

    Natural stones and 925 silver on elastic cord.

    Clear quartz, smoky quartz, rose quartz, onyx: familiar stones cut into calibrated rounds and punctuated with 925 silver. The elastic cord makes each bracelet easy to slip on, with no clasp.

    from €69 · indicative price

  2. Glass & Glaze

    Glass, glazed ceramic and lampwork, paced with 316L steel.

    Colour first. Tinted or frosted glass, glazed ceramics and a flame-worked glass bead as the focal point. 316L steel adds a graphic note and keeps the price accessible.

    from €55 · indicative price

  3. Pearl

    Freshwater pearls and 925 silver.

    Freshwater cultured pearls with slightly irregular shapes, paired with silver or onyx. Every pearl is different, and that is what makes them feel alive.

    from €79 · indicative price

  4. Structure

    A 925 silver chain or half-bangle joined to a beaded section.

    One half supple, the other half built. A cable chain or a 925 silver half-bangle, joined to a beaded section with jump rings and crimp covers. A more architectural line, worn alone or stacked.

    from €119 · indicative price
