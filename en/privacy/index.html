In short

The site works without an account, advertising or audience measurement. It sets no tracking cookies. Fonts are served from the site itself, so displaying a page makes no request to a third-party service.

What stays on your device

Your language, appearance preference and the compositions you save are kept in your browser’s local storage. They are never sent to us. You can delete them from the Atelier or in your browser settings.

The photo used for the virtual try-on is processed only in your browser. ORIMEA neither receives nor stores it.

Start from an intention

What you enter in “Start from an intention” (intention, day and month, year, date and time of birth, sentence) is processed only in your browser. It is not sent to us and not kept: when you save a composition, only the beads are kept, never the date or the sentence.

AI mode, off by default. Only if this mode is on and you click “Refine with AI” is your sentence sent to our provider OpenRouter (United States), which passes it to the Mistral Small 3.2 model to sort it into intentions and colours: first to its publisher, Mistral AI (France), otherwise to another host of that model. The mode is set to use only providers that, by OpenRouter’s classification, do not collect user data (no lasting retention, no model training). ORIMEA does not record it or write it to any log. To limit abuse, the number of sentences sent is counted per IP address over one minute, in a temporary counter. Legal basis: your consent, given by that click (GDPR article 6(1)(a)). Please do not write health information or other sensitive data in it. Retention by the providers and safeguards for the transfer outside the European Union: [to be checked in the terms of OpenRouter and Mistral AI before the mode is turned on].

Emails you send us

When you reserve a composition or write to us, your email app sends us your email address, your name if included, your message and the details of the composition.

Purpose: to answer your request and, if you wish, prepare an offer. Legal basis: pre-contractual steps taken at your request (GDPR article 6(1)(b)).

Retention: [to be completed, e.g. 3 years after the last exchange]. Recipients: the ORIMEA team and its email provider [to be completed].

Hosting

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA — +1 (650) 319-8930 — cloudflare.com. The host processes technical logs (including IP addresses) to deliver pages and keep them secure. According to its privacy policy (4 November 2025), Cloudflare adheres to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and otherwise relies on standard contractual clauses [to be confirmed in Cloudflare’s data processing agreement (DPA)].

Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability. Write to contact@orimea.example. You may also complain to the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).
