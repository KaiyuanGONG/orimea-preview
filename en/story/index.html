Our story

A bracelet is composed like a sentence.

We wear on our wrist what we keep close: a date, a journey, a colour we love. We wanted a bracelet you compose the way you choose your words, bead by bead, free to change your mind. ORIMEA grew from that idea: to put a workshop’s tools in your hands, with the precision of a well-made jewel.

The name

In ORIMEA you can hear a rhyme: the rhythm beads trace around the wrist, an alternation, a return, an accent.

What we make

Four lines, one method. Mineral for stones, Glass & Glaze for colour, Pearl for soft light, Structure for more constructed lines. In the online Atelier every bead can be replaced, the size fits your wrist and the price updates as you go.

What we believe

  • The right size

    A bracelet feels right when it is the right length. We work out the number of beads from your wrist and the fit you prefer.

  • Materials as they are

    Natural stone, glass, ceramic, cultured pearl, 925 silver, 316L steel: we say what it is, and what it is not. We attribute no powers to stones.

  • Drawing before ornament

    Few elements, a legible rhythm, one focal point. Every design fits in a sentence.

Designed in France

ORIMEA designs are drawn in France, to be worn every day, alone or stacked. We work with workshops and suppliers chosen for the consistency of their materials. Every product sheet will state the exact nature of the material and any treatment.

Where we are

ORIMEA is preparing its launch. The images on this site are visualisations of our reference compositions; photographs of the first samples will replace them. In the meantime you can compose and reserve your bracelet without paying anything: we will come back to you with the final price and lead time.
