Frequently asked questions

Can I buy now?

Not yet. ORIMEA is preparing its launch: you can compose your bracelet and reserve it without paying. We come back to you with the final price and lead time.

Is a reservation binding?

No. A reservation is a request for information. Nothing is owed until you confirm an order under the final terms.

Are the images photographs?

They are 3D renders computed from each material’s dimensions and optical properties. Photographs of the first samples will replace them.

Are the prices final?

They are indicative. The final price is confirmed to you before any order.

How do I choose my size?

Measure your wrist and choose a fit; the Atelier works out the number of beads. The “Find your size” page explains the method.

Do the stones have special properties?

We choose our stones for their colour, clarity and feel. We attribute no effect on health, luck or mood to them.

I am sensitive to metals.

Our metals are 925 silver and 316L stainless steel. If you have a known allergy, write to us before reserving and we will tell you the exact composition of each part.

Guide · 925 silver and 316L steel: what they are, how to care for them

Is the elastic cord strong?

We use a clear, jewellery-grade elastic cord. Roll the bracelet over your hand rather than stretching it, and take it off for water and sport.

Can I share my composition?

Yes. The Atelier creates a link that contains the whole composition; it opens exactly as you left it on any device.

Delivery and returns?

The terms will be published at launch, for France and the European Union. Please note that jewellery made to your specifications may be excluded from the right of withdrawal (French Consumer Code, article L221-28); our terms will state what applies.
