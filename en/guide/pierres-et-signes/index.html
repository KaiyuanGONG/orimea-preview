Stones and zodiac signs: nine lists, no agreement

We compared nine lists in French, English and Chinese that pair a stone with each sign. No stone appears on all of them. What that says about these lists, and the rule we follow.

Updated September 30, 2026 · 3 min readUnder review

In brief

  • Across nine lists, no stone is shared by all of them, for any of the twelve signs.
  • Depending on the sign, the lists offer between 15 and 24 different stones in total.
  • So we pair colours with the four elements, and show a French astrology site’s list alongside.

Contents

  1. Five of these lists side by side
  2. What the comparison shows
  3. Our rule

Many shops and websites offer “the stone for your sign”. Before choosing our rule, we compared nine of these lists, as they stood on 30 September 2026: three French sites (Karmastro, Michaël Vessière, Valentin&Valentine), a Geneva jeweller (BenGems), two English-language jewellers (Brilliant Earth, Royal Coster), two Chinese articles on the Sina portal (Sina Art, 易学师论) and Wikipedia.

To count, we grouped varieties under their stone (rubellite is a tourmaline, for example); other choices would move the totals by a few, not the conclusion.

What the comparison shows

  • No full agreement. For no sign does the same stone appear on all nine lists.
  • Partial agreement. The closest matches are moonstone for Cancer and aquamarine for Pisces, each named by six lists out of nine, by our count.
  • Same portal, different stones. The two articles on Sina give a different stone for every one of the twelve signs.
  • The month calendar underneath. At Brilliant Earth, each sign gets the American birthstone of the month in which it ends: diamond for Aries (April), garnet for Capricorn (January)… This is our own observation: the page does not say so.

A French jeweller, Trabbia Vuillermoz, sums it up: attributions “can differ from one tradition to another”. The Wikipedia table, often copied, cites no source; for the history of these correspondences, the encyclopedia relies on George F. Kunz’s book (1913), which we could not consult.

Most of these pages also attribute powers to the stones. We do not repeat them.

Our rule

In Western astrology, the twelve signs are traditionally grouped into four elements. Rather than pick one list among many, we give each element a colour palette:

Element Signs Colours Stones in our range
Fire Aries, Leo, Sagittarius red and orange carnelian, garnet
Earth Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn pink, green and brown rose quartz, chrysoprase, smoky quartz
Air Gemini, Libra, Aquarius white, blue and yellow clear quartz, aquamarine, citrine
Water Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces sea blue and pearl aquamarine, moonstone, cultured freshwater pearl

Alongside, we show the list of the French astrology site Karmastro (the first column of the table above). It is not an official list: there is none.

This is a traditional correspondence, not a birth chart: we compute no ascendant and no planetary positions. Sign dates vary by one day depending on the source; we follow those on How we talk about meaning.

In the Atelier, Start from an intention suggests these colours from your date of birth. All the stones mentioned are virtual for now.

These associations come from traditions and beliefs; they have no proven effect. No stone heals or guarantees any outcome.
